About GESE Premier 2026 Ltd

GESE Premier 2026 Ltd (Geomatic Survey & Engineering Premier 2026 Limited) is a professional land surveying and engineering consultancy company based in Rwanda. The company provides accurate, reliable, and modern geomatic and engineering survey services to support land administration, infrastructure development, and spatial planning.

Our mission is to provide precise, efficient, and affordable surveying and engineering services using modern technologies while maintaining professionalism, integrity, and client satisfaction. We support government institutions, private developers, landowners, and local communities with dependable geospatial solutions.
